A writer thought a task through aloud and then made a series of decisions, each between two versions
of a text. Name the high-level preferences this shows about how they like to write, in a way that would
also hold for a different text (not "Lead with the nurses" but "Leads with the human impact").

Return three to six preferences, only as many as what they said and chose supports. Each: topic, one
to three words (for example "Starting point", "Tone", "Arguments", "Weak points", "Length"); preference,
at most eight words. Prefer what they said about their own way of working, then what their decisions
show. No details of this task, no duplicates.
